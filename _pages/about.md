Hi, I'm Shenghan Gao, an M.S.E. student in Computer Science and Technology at the [School of Information Science and Technology](https://sist.shanghaitech.edu.cn/sist_en/), [ShanghaiTech University](https://www.shanghaitech.edu.cn/eng/), where I began my master's studies in September 2026 under the supervision of Prof. [Quan Li](https://faculty.sist.shanghaitech.edu.cn/liquan/). I received my B.Eng. from ShanghaiTech in June 2025 and was an exchange student in EECS at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/) in Fall 2023.

My research focuses on **Human-Centered Computing**, particularly **Human-AI Collaboration**, **Human-Computer Interaction**, and **Data Visualization**. I combine qualitative interviews and quantitative behavioral analysis to understand users' mental models, and design interactive systems that manage cognitive load and support human decision-making. My goal is to bridge complex technological systems and human cognition to enhance human well-being.

I have been a research intern at [ViSeer LAB](https://faculty.sist.shanghaitech.edu.cn/liquan/) since December 2022. My work includes **SCSimulator** (IUI 2026), which supports supply-chain partner selection through LLM-driven multi-agent simulation, and **CommSense** (CHI 2026, co-first author), which helps users navigate online comments with greater bias awareness and reflective reasoning. See my [publications](/publications/) and [CV](/cv/) for more details.

Outside research, I enjoy history and strategy games, especially Paradox titles such as Europa Universalis IV!
